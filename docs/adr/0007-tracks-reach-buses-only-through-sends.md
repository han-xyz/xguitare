# A Project has a fixed mixer: Tracks go to Master and reach Buses only through Sends

A Project's mixer has one fixed shape, and it is the design, not a step towards something else. Each Track plays its Items or its input through its own Rack, pan and volume, and goes to Master unless its `to_master` is off. A Track reaches a Bus only through a Send, taken before the Track's volume (`pre`) or after it, at its own level; a Track has at most one Send per Bus and a Project at most eight Buses. Each Bus runs what its Sends bring through its own stereo Rack, volume and pan into Master. Master runs the sum through its own stereo Rack and volume, and its output is what you hear and what a Render writes. Mute and solo in place act on Tracks and Buses. Nothing else connects stages: a Bus does not send to a Bus, a Track does not play into a Track, and no output goes back to an earlier stage. The audio thread's `Arrangement` (`crates/xguitare-core/src/arrangement.rs`) runs this shape over fixed arrays; the Control API changes it with `track_set` and with `send_set`, `bus_add`, `bus_set`, `bus_remove` and `master_set` (`crates/xguitare-core/src/mixer_control.rs`), and `project.toml` stores it as Tracks with their `sends`, `buses` and `master`. A Rack stays a linear Module list (ADR-0002); the graph ADR-0002 expected with the DAW is not coming.

Considered and rejected: a free routing graph, where any Track, Bus or Rack output can be connected to any input, with splits, merges and feedback guards. It would make real-time safety a property each user-built graph must have (an order computed per change, cycles refused, buffers sized per edge), where the fixed shape has it by structure: the order is always Tracks, Buses, Master, and every buffer is sized when the Arrangement is built. It would also lose the mixer the GUI shows: one column per Track, then per Bus, then Master, each with its pan, fader, meter, mute, solo and Sends; a graph needs a patching view instead. And no user need for such a graph has been named: a shared reverb, a Module run in parallel with the dry Track and a submix are each a Bus with Sends.

What it costs. A connection the shape does not hold, such as a Bus into another Bus, a sidechain or one Track's output recorded onto another, cannot be built; it needs a new decision that supersedes this one. Each new kind of stage (a group of Buses, for example) is a change to the Arrangement, the `project.toml` format and the mixer view, not a configuration.
