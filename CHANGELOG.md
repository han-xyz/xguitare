# Changelog

All notable changes to xguitare are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## Unreleased

### Added

- Control API versioning: `status` carries `protocol_version` (1), which goes up only when a field is removed or changes meaning; `xguitare status` prints it and warns on stderr when the Daemon's is newer, and the GUI shows a notice once per connection. Error replies may carry a machine-readable `code`, so far `still_closing`; the GUI retries opening a Project on that code, and on the old text from a Daemon without codes.
- Items have an id (Control API `items` `id`, first on each `project items` line) and may have a `length`; where Items overlap on a Track the one on top (the newest) is heard, and Item edges fade over 5 ms with a crossfade where one Item cuts into another. A `project.toml` without Item ids loads with ids in file order.
- Editing Items: CLI `item list|move|trim|split|delete|take` and Control API `item_move`, `item_trim`, `item_split`, `item_delete` and `item_take`; moved Items go on top, a split keeps all Takes on both halves, and while playing the changed Tracks cross over to the edit in 5 ms without rebuilding their Racks.
- Project undo and redo: CLI `project undo|redo` and Control API `project_undo` and `project_redo` take back and do again the last 200 Item edits, recorded Takes and Track adds and removes of the open Project (a removed Track comes back with its Rack, mixer values and Items); `status` shows the `undo` and `redo` labels.
- Duplicate, copy and paste Items: CLI `item duplicate|copy|paste` and Control API `item_duplicate`, `item_copy` and `item_paste`; copies keep their Takes, Track distance and spacing and go on top; the clipboard lives in the Daemon until the Project closes, `status` counts it in `clipboard`, and a duplicate or paste is one undo entry.
- GUI Item editing in `Arrange`: click, Ctrl+click and a box on an empty lane select Items (amber outline); dragging moves the selection, snapped to the drawn grid while `Snap` is on (Alt turns it the other way, Shift keeps the Tracks), with a grey plate where each Item lands, sent as one `item_move` on release; `S` splits at the playhead, `Delete`/`Backspace` deletes, `←` `→` move by one grid step and `Alt+↑` `Alt+↓` to the next Track, `Enter`/`Ctrl+Enter` select the focused Item, `Esc` clears the selection, and `Ctrl+Z`/`Ctrl+Shift+Z` undo and redo in every Project view; the footer shows the next undo (`Undo: move 2 Items  Ctrl+Z`). Items are keyboard stops named `Item <Take>, <Track>, bar <n>`.
- GUI trimming, Takes and copies in `Arrange`: drag an Item's edge to trim it (snapped, inside its Take; `item_trim` on release) or press `Shift+←`/`Shift+→` (end) and `Ctrl+Shift+←`/`Ctrl+Shift+→` (start) on a focused Item; right-click an Item (or `Shift+F10`) for its Takes, `Split at playhead`, `Duplicate`, `Copy` and `Delete`, and an empty lane for `Paste`; `T` makes the next Take active, `Ctrl+D` duplicates, `Ctrl+C` copies and `Ctrl+V` pastes at the playhead on the selected Track, the new Items selected. Where Items overlap, only the top one, the one heard, is drawn there: the lower one shows as its uncovered stretches, its name once. A GUI copy also puts `xguitare Items: <Take names>` on the system clipboard. Item keyboard names add the Take count and what covers them.
- Waveforms: the GUI draws each Item's waveform, for the part of its active Take it plays, on its plate in the Track's colour. Control API `take_peaks` pages a Take's peak table (min and max per 256 frames, up to 16384 pairs a reply, with the Take's `frames`); `items` gives each Item's `take_frames` and `status` Tracks their `id`.
- Clean up Takes: CLI `takes unused` lists the open Project's Take files that no Item, the clipboard or a recording uses, with sizes and a total, and `takes clean --yes` deletes them (without `--yes` it only lists); Control API `takes_unused` and `takes_clean`. Deleting saves the Project first and clears the undo history and the clipboard; it is refused while recording, a symlink in `takes/` is never followed, and a file that cannot be deleted is reported without stopping the rest; the CLI and the GUI send a long list in batches. The GUI's `Project` section has `Clean up Takes…`. Take files are otherwise never deleted.
- `autowah` Module, an envelope filter for bass and guitar: the input's envelope sweeps a low-, band- or high-pass filter between `freq_low` and `freq_high`, up or down, with `sensitivity`, `attack`, `decay`, `q`, `dry` and `wet`.
- `eq` Module, a parametric equaliser: 12 dB/oct `low_cut` and `high_cut`, a low and a high shelf, two bells with their own `q`, and `output`.
- `blend` Module, a bass DI blend drive: below `crossover` the signal stays clean, above it `presence` and `drive` clip it at `level`; `blend` mixes the clean input with that, and `bass` (40 or 80 Hz), `mid` (500 or 1000 Hz) and `treble` shape the result, also at `blend` 0.
- `limiter` Module, a peak limiter for the end of a Rack, a Bus or the Master: `input` drives it, the output never exceeds `ceiling`, `release` sets the recovery and `lookahead` (0–5 ms) delays the sound so a peak is caught before it passes; on a Bus or the Master both sides share one gain.
- `shaper` Module, a transient shaper: `attack` raises or lowers the start of each note and `sustain` what follows, with no threshold, plus `output` and `mix`; on a Bus or the Master both sides share one gain.
- `octave` Module, a tracking octaver for single notes with no added delay: `oct1` one octave down, `oct2` two octaves down and `up` one octave up beside `dry`, `tone` on the two sub voices, `range` bass|guitar.
- Metal starter Presets in both starter Banks: Thrash, Djent, Doom, Buzzsaw and Stoner (programs 8 to 12); an existing Bank is not touched, copy the files from `crates/xguitare-core/bank/` or `bank-bass/` to add them.
- Bass starter Presets Envelope Funk (compressor, `autowah`, `eq`, cabinet) and Modern Grind (`eq`, `blend`, `limiter`, cabinet); an existing bass Bank is not touched.
- Starter Presets Sub Octave (`octave` with only the octave below) and Distortion (`drive` into a clean `preamp`) in the guitar Bank, Synth Sub (`octave` under the bass) and Distortion (`blend`) in the bass Bank, programs 13 and 14, matched in loudness like the others; an existing Bank is not touched, copy the files from `crates/xguitare-core/bank/` or `bank-bass/` to add them.
- GUI: the `eq` unit (`EQ`) shows its response curve, a small one when closed and, when open, a plot with a dot per band over four band columns of knobs.
- GUI: a choice Parameter is a row of buttons in an open unit and a chip with the current choice in a closed one (a click on a two-way chip switches it); an open unit grows to hold its knobs, which wrap to another row between groups of Parameters.
- Choice Parameters: the Control API `modules` and `get` entries carry `labels` (`["lp","bp","hp"]`; `[]` for a plain number) and `xguitare modules` prints them, `mode (lp|bp|hp)`. Presets and `set` keep the number.
- Metronome: a blip on every beat with beat 1 higher and 6 dB louder, at its own tempo (20–300 bpm), meter and volume (−40 to 0 dB) with no Project open, and as the Project's click with one open; tap tempo; Mute silences it while it keeps counting; tempo, meter and volume are kept in `state.toml`. CLI `metronome on|off|tempo|volume|tap`; Control API `metronome` and `metronome_tap`; `status` shows `metronome`. The GUI's `Metronome` section, with a beat light, and the `M` (on/off) and `T` (tap) keys.
- A GitHub Actions release workflow builds `xguitare` and `xguitare-gui` for x86_64 Linux on a `v*` tag and attaches a tarball and its sha256 to the GitHub Release. The tarball holds `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `LICENSE` and `OFL.txt`, the licence of the bundled IBM Plex fonts.
- Render a Project to a stereo WAV in the background (`renders/<name>.wav`, never replacing a file): the whole Project with its tail, or the loop region; 24-bit, 16-bit with dither or 32-bit float. CLI `render [--loop] [--bits 16|24|32f] [--name <name>]` and `render cancel`; Control API `render` and `render_cancel`; `status` shows `render`.
- Mixer in a Project: up to 8 Buses with stereo Racks, a Send per Track and Bus (post- or pre-fader), a Master Rack and volume, `to master` per Track, solo in place, and output peaks with a latching clip flag per Track, Bus and Master. CLI `bus add|remove|edit`, `send`, `master`, `track edit --to-master`, `clip reset`, and `--bus <bus>` / `--master` on `rack`, `set`, `get`, `bypass` and `ir set`; Control API `bus_add`, `bus_remove`, `bus_set`, `send_set`, `master_set`, `clip_reset`, `track_set` `to_master`, and `bus` / `master` on the Rack requests; `status` shows `buses`, `master` and each Track's `to_master`, `sends`, `peak_db` and `clip`.
- Projects: record and play back up to 16 Tracks, each with its own Rack, input, volume, pan, mute, solo and arm, with tempo, meter, loop, click and count-in. CLI `project new|open|save|close|list`, `track add|remove|edit|select`, `play`, `stop`, `record`, `locate`, `tempo`, `click`; Control API `project_*`, `track_*`, `transport`, `loop`, `tempo`, `click`, and an optional `track` on the Rack requests. Takes are placed latency-compensated; an open Project is autosaved every 60 seconds while changed and when the Daemon stops.
- `status` shows `disk_overflows`, `count_in_remaining`, `loaded_audio_bytes`, `project_warning` and, while a Project is open, its transport, position, tempo, loop, click and Tracks; `settings` and `set_settings` take `projects_dir`.
- The Control API `items` request and CLI `project items` list each Track's Items; `track_select` selects a Track.
- The GUI turns into a Project view while a Project is open: transport bar, Track rows, a timeline with the Items, loop region and playhead, and the selected Track's Rack.
- First run creates 8 guitar and 8 bass starter Presets (Rock, Blues, Funk; bass Rock); existing Banks are not touched.
- `get` and `get <module>` end each Module's lines with `<id>.bypass = yes|no`.
- The Daemon follows a sample-rate change of the audio server: it rebuilds the Rack (Cabinet IRs resampled) and the Tuner at the new rate, logs `sample rate changed: 48000 -> 96000`, and `status` shows the new `sample_rate`.
- `get` on a cabinet ends with its Cabinet IR, `cabinet.ir = default-cab.wav` (`none` when it has none).
- The Daemon serves at most 32 connections at once; one more gets `too many connections` and is closed. A connection idle for 300 seconds is closed.
- Bypass per Module: `xguitare bypass <id> on|off` and the Control API `bypass` request switch one Module to passing its input through, with a 10 ms crossfade; a Preset's `[[module]]` takes `bypass = true`, `save` writes it, and `get` entries carry `bypass`.

### Changed

- Starter Presets: the cabinet `level` of all 26 starter Presets is retuned so that on a plucked test signal at −18 dB RMS none has a true peak above −1 dBTP and the Presets of a Bank play at one BS.1770 loudness (−13 LUFS guitar, −14 LUFS bass; bass Primus Flange and Envelope Funk sit lower, at the ceiling); an existing Bank is not touched, copy the files from `crates/xguitare-core/bank/` or `bank-bass/` to take the new levels.
- Starter Presets: guitar Stoner has an `octave` adding the octave below before its `drive`, bass Stoner one adding the octave up before its `blend`; bass Stoner's cabinet `level` is −18.0 dB (was −17.9).
- GUI: the meter bridge and the Tuner unit can be dragged to any gap of the Rack, moved with their `↑` `↓`, or hidden with `Hide` or by dragging them out (the footer offers Undo); the `Tuner` and `Meters` chips under `Add Module` show them again. Their places are kept across restarts and shared by every Rack the window shows.
- GUI: the left panel's `Metronome` section now sits above `Add Module`, so it is in view at 1280×800.
- GUI: new "Faceplate" look, part 1: one theme for every colour and text size, the IBM Plex fonts bundled in the binary, and a new set of eighteen Module colours, each distinct from the others and readable with dark text on it.
- GUI: "Faceplate" look, part 2: knobs with a printed tick ring and a value arc in the Module colour, ringed lamps, hover and pressed states on every button, a white focus ring on whichever control has the keyboard focus, text fields with a lit edge while editing, and a bypassed unit drawn in grey with a `Bypassed` tag instead of faded. A group of choice buttons is now one `Tab` stop.
- GUI: "Faceplate" look, part 3: each Rack unit is a faceplate between steel ears engraved with its Rack position and edged in its Module colour; the meter bridge has a dB scale; the Tuner unit has its own `Off`/`On` switch and a cents strip; the EQ plot sits in a recessed well beside its band columns, and a closed EQ lists its bands (`+4 @100 −3 @400`). `Remove` moved next to `↑` `↓` in an open unit.
- GUI: "Faceplate" look for the Project view: the transport bar and the Tracks are faceplates between steel ears, the position sits in a dark display in large digits, Play, Record, Loop, Click and the Track switches fill with their colour while on, each Track header has a colour tab and its number, and Items are dark plates with a Track-colour top edge; the Take being recorded is a dashed red plate growing to the playhead.
- GUI: with a Project open the main area shows one of three views, `Rack`, `Arrange` (Tracks and timeline over the Rack) or `Mixer`, chosen in the transport bar or with Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2, Ctrl+3; Ctrl+M shows the mixer and goes back to the view before it. The mixer no longer shares the window with a Rack: clicking a strip's name picks the Rack that `Rack` and `Arrange` show, and Enter on a focused strip name shows that Rack in `Rack`. The last view is kept across restarts. The mixer strips take the window's height: faders from 134 up to 320 pixels, and a Send row per Bus while there is room.
- GUI: "Faceplate" look for dialogs and states: the Render dialog and the autosave prompt sit on a raised panel over the darkened window, with the main action as a light button; the Render progress bar fills in amber; a failed Render shows its error in red with a warning sign; the Mute banner carries a struck-through speaker. While no Daemon answers, an amber-edged `Daemon disconnected` (or `Starting the Daemon`) banner above the rack replaces the footer's `disconnected Ns`, and the units turn grey and take no input. Errors show in a toast at the bottom right with a red edge, the action that failed over the Daemon's reason, until dismissed with its `✕` or Esc or replaced by the next error, instead of in the footer for 3 seconds. The Render dialog's selected radio is lit amber, and a unit's `▶` is a full 24-pixel target with a hover state. `Save as new` puts the text cursor in its name field. The left panel scrolls at 1280×800 to reach the `Metronome` below the 18 Module tiles.
- The Project click has an accent: beat 1 is 6 dB louder than the other beats, and the click plays at the Metronome volume (default −12 dB), quieter than before.
- Cabinet IRs are normalised to unit energy on load, so Banks created before this change sound about 12 dB quieter. Raise the cabinet `level` in your Presets (about 12, maximum 20); the starter Presets carry `level = 12`.
- A stereo Cabinet IR is normalised over both channels, so it plays as loud in total as a mono IR; a right-only IR is no longer boosted.
- Only the first `ir_max_ms` of a Cabinet IR is read.
- `set` rounds the value of an integer Parameter (`preamp.stages`, `delay.pingpong`, `cabinet.low_cut`, `cabinet.high_cut`) before clamping, so `set cabinet.low_cut 150.4` answers and saves 150.
- Mute fades over 10 ms at any sample rate.
- A Preset switch that arrives during a crossfade waits for it to end and then crossfades; of several, only the newest runs.
- `status` peaks are kept per connection, so the GUI and the CLI no longer reset each other's.
- `save` keeps the Preset file's permissions and writes through a symlink to its target.
- The Daemon keeps running when a GUI that started it crashes; closing the window cleanly still stops it.
- A second `xguitare run` on the same socket exits with `error: another Daemon is running` (it was `error: daemon already running at <socket>`); the running Daemon holds a lock on `<socket>.lock`, e.g. `xguitare.sock.lock`.
- `xguitare rack` with no subcommand prints one `error:` line and exits 1.

### Fixed

- A Project file holding an Item whose active Take does not play past its `offset` (which a stop just after a loop wrap could record) opens with that Item playing the newest of its Takes that does, keeping all its Takes, and warns `track '<name>': item <id> now plays <take file>: <old take file> ends before the item starts`; an Item none of whose Takes plays is left out with `track '<name>': dropped item <id> (<take files>): starts past the end of its take`, its Take files staying on disk. The warnings are in the `project_open` reply, the Daemon log, the CLI's stderr and the GUI's footer. Saving such an Item is refused with `track '<name>' item <id> starts past the end of its take`.
- `locate` is bounded by where each Item really ends (its active Take less its `offset`), no longer by its longest Take.
- A Cabinet IR with a NaN or infinite sample is refused with `ir <name>: non-finite sample`.
- `rack_add cabinet` finds `default-cab.wav` or `bass-cab.wav` under the IR directory as well as the Bank directory.
- An IR path error names both directories it looked in.
- `crossfade_ms` (above 0 to 1000) and `ir_max_ms` (above 0 to 10000) out of range, NaN or infinite are refused when the config loads.
- A preamp `stages` change no longer jumps in level.
- The CLI and the GUI refuse a fallback socket directory that is not yours or not mode 0700.
- A Daemon the GUI started that exits before it answers (a bad config key, JACK not running) is no longer started again every 5 s behind a `Daemon disconnected` banner: the rack shows `The Daemon exited` with the Daemon's last stderr line, and the GUI waits for a Daemon you start.

### Known

- Some crates under eframe/winit are unmaintained: ttf-parser (RUSTSEC-2026-0192) and zbus_names, zvariant, zvariant_derive (RUSTSEC-2026-0370, RUSTSEC-2026-0371, RUSTSEC-2026-0372). They are transitive; nothing to do until egui moves off them.
